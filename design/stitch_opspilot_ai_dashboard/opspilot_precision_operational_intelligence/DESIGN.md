## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated precision, clarity, and speed of next-generation developer and operations tooling—inspired directly by the utilitarian elegance of Linear and the refined clarity of Vercel. 

### Brand Personality & Mood
- **Hyper-focused & Instrument-grade:** The interface is engineered as an analytical console. It avoids ornamental decoration in favor of high-density clarity, structured typographic hierarchies, and surgical data presentation.
- **Empathetic Calm:** By combining structured cool slate-navies with humanized Sand and Cream tones, the operational cockpit remains easy on the eyes during prolonged triage sessions.
- **Predictive & Intelligent:** AI diagnoses and suggested responses emerge naturally as elevated surface tiers rather than jarring interventions, using subtle warm indicators to signify augmentative machine assistance.

### Design Style
Minimalist Modern with structural micro-borders, generous geometric whitespace, calibrated line heights, and muted surface layering. Lucide icons provide continuous 1.5px stroke coherence across every viewport.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy in this system leverages optical tracking adjustments inspired by Linear's dense, high-legibility UI. Inter is deployed across all UI surfaces with distinct kerning shifts: negative tracking for headings to build gravity, and neutral-to-positive tracking for operational labels and diagnostic micro-copy.

### Implementation Rules
- Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be activated for ticket IDs, metric counts, timestamps, and confidence percentages.
- `label-sm` utilizes uppercase formatting exclusively for category tags, workflow steps, and severity chips.
- Body paragraphs maintain a maximum line length of 68 characters to prevent eye fatigue during lengthy incident assessments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a hybrid workspace model: a fixed 240px utility sidebar with a fluid, multi-pane triage canvas structured on a 12-column grid.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 3-pane layout (Navigation, Ticket Stream, AI Diagnostic Inspector). Outer margins set to `margin` (2rem), gutters set to `gutter` (1.5rem).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 2-pane layout (Navigation collapses to icon-rail; ticket stream and AI inspector toggle or stack vertically). Gutters adjust to `gutter-sm` (1rem).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with full-width drawers for diagnostic views. Outer margin compresses to `margin-sm` (1rem).
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** High component padding (`space-lg`) prevents data overload. Dense metadata clusters inside cards rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for unified grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use deep, harsh drop shadows. Instead, it relies on hairline borders paired with diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with `navy-900`.

### Surface & Border Rules
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `cream-400` flat background.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Workspaces):** `cream-100` with border `1px solid #E3E5E7` (`navy-100`).
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Panels & Flyouts):** `cream-50` with border `1px solid #C4C9CE` (`navy-200`) and shadow `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 38, 58, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Command K Palettes):** `cream-50` with border `1px solid #9BA4AC` (`navy-300`) and shadow `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 38, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Ghost Outlines:** All interactive cards transition their border color to `teal-500` or `navy-300` on hover, maintaining a 1px footprint without layout jitter.

## Shapes

The geometric signature of the design system is calibrated strictly to rounded-xl corners (12px / 0.75rem) for major containers, maintaining tactile polish across dense views.

### Hierarchy of Radii
- **Containers, Cards, Dialogs, AI Panels:** `12px` (`rounded-xl` token mapping).
- **Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`) for sharp, responsive target precision.
- **Badges, Inline Status Tags, Keyboard Shortcut Pills:** `6px` (`rounded-md`) or full pill (`9999px`) for discrete state counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `teal-500` (`#06BCC1`), text `navy-900` (`#12263A`), font weight 600, border radius 8px, padding 10px 16px. Hover: background `teal-600`. Active: background `teal-700`. Text must never be white.
- **Secondary:** Background `cream-100`, border `1px solid #E3E5E7`, text `navy-900`. Hover: background `navy-50`, border `navy-300`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `navy-500`. Hover: background `cream-300`, text `navy-900`.
- **Icon Pairing:** Lucide icons sized to 16px with 1.5px stroke width, spaced 8px from text labels.

### AI Suggested Response Panel
- **Container:** Background `sand-100` (`#FDF7F0`), border `1px solid #F9E6D3` (`sand-300`), border radius `12px`, padding 20px.
- **Header:** Sparkle icon (Lucide) in `sand-600` alongside an "OpsPilot Suggestion" tag in `label-sm`.
- **Accents:** Highlight pills or confidence indicators rendered with background `sand-500` text `navy-900`.
- **Action Footers:** Quick-insert and edit actions utilize ghost buttons with `sand-600` hover states.

### Cards & Ticket Rows
- **List Items / Ticket Rows:** Background `cream-100`, border bottom `1px solid #E3E5E7`, padding 12px 16px. Transitions to `navy-50` on hover with a 2px left border accent in `teal-500` when selected.
- **Grid Cards:** Rounded 12px, border `1px solid #E3E5E7`, ambient shadow `rgba(18, 38, 58, 0.08)`.

### Priority Badges
- 11px font size, semi-bold, uppercase, 6px radius, padding 2px 8px.
- **Critical:** Background `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.12)`, text `#DC2626`.
- **High:** Background `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.12)`, text `#EA580C`.
- **Medium:** Background `rgba(202, 138, 4, 0.12)`, text `#CA8A04`.
- **Low:** Background `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.12)`, text `#16A34A`.

### Input Fields & Search (Linear-style)
- Background `cream-50`, border `1px solid #E3E5E7`, text `navy-900`, placeholder `navy-300`, radius 8px, padding 8px 12px.
- Focus state: Border color `teal-500` with ring `0 0 0 1px #06BCC1`. No heavy native rings.
- Inline shortcuts displayed with a right-aligned kbd token (background `cream-200`, border `navy-200`, text `navy-400`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 16x16px boxes, 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.

- Checked: Background `navy-900`, border `navy-900`, checkmark icon in `teal-400`.
